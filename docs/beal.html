Beal's conjecture

Aˣ + Bʸ = Cᶻ (x,y,z > 2)

For positive integers satisfying this equation, must the three bases share a prime divisor?

Study generalized Fermat signatures, explicit auxiliary curves, local obstructions and the hypotheses needed for a global descent.
