Standards of evidence

  1. Specify the exact statement, quantifiers and model.
  2. Distinguish exact certificates, analytic deductions, conditional claims, numerical exploration and rejected hypotheses.
  3. Report a reconstruction as a reconstruction, not an original discovery or the recovery of missing code.
  4. Retain negative results and explicit open steps. Invite independent criticism.
  5. Never equate computation speed with proof, finite tests with asymptotics, or local solubility with global solubility.

All notes are AI-assisted and not peer reviewed. No affiliation, priority or prize claim is made. Novelty requires independent literature review.
