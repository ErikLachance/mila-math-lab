P vs NP

P = NP ?

Does every decision problem with polynomial-time verifiable certificates admit a deterministic polynomial-time decision algorithm?

Study restricted proof systems, Boolean maps, and arithmetic-to-bit complexity transfers. Finite enumeration is not a general SAT algorithm.

ML-PNP-005P vs NP
Proof and finite verification
Verified 26 Nov 2026

Graph algebraic immunity: exact threshold benchmarks and limits of repetition

Self-contained proofs for threshold graphs and Cartesian products, applied to the finite-generator research program.

Result. Odd majority on 2k−1 inputs has exact graph relation degree k; repeating independent blocks preserves the minimum degree.

Scope. Majority has small circuits. A growing relation degree alone gives no general circuit lower bound or P vs NP separation. Established concepts; no novelty claim.
