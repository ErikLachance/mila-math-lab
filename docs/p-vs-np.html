P vs NP

P = NP ?

Does every decision problem with polynomial-time verifiable certificates admit a deterministic polynomial-time decision algorithm?

Study restricted proof systems, Boolean maps, and arithmetic-to-bit complexity transfers. Finite enumeration is not a general SAT algorithm.
