Exact integer recursion for fixed rational bilinear matrix identities

A denominator-clearing construction and explicit bit-length bound for recursive block multiplication.

Result. H=C M²(ABb)ʰ; 96 exact comparisons and 36,576 exact divisions, without guard violations.

Scope. The identity is fixed. Uniform fast PIT construction and cutoff remain open.

ML-BEAL-004Beal's conjecture
Method obstruction
Rechecked 26 Nov 2026

Valuation obstructions to the rational-root reduction for (3,4,11)

The auxiliary polynomial has a restricted rational image despite its real surjectivity on the unit interval.

Result. 1/2 has no rational preimage. The conditional branch cannot cover primitive solutions with C divisible by 2 or 3. 20,087 coprime pairs checked.

Scope. Obstruction to the method, not a nonexistence theorem for the original equation.

ML-RH-004Riemann hypothesis
Analytic obstruction
Rechecked 26 Nov 2026

A Carleman obstruction at a spatial interface in the Weil form

A scale-invariant archimedean singularity between touching spatial supports.

Result. The absolute mixed form has supremum at least π/2 on normalized smooth tests for every positive strip width.

Scope. Rules out vanishing unweighted spatial cross-block estimates, not Weil positivity or spectral projection methods.
