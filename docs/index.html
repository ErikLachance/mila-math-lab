Exact certificate
Rechecked 26 Nov 2026

Local arithmetic of 36 auxiliary ternary cubics

Direct cubic-ring arithmetic and strong Hensel certificates distinguish obstructions from local points.

Result. Nine forms have no ℚ₃ point; 27 have certified ℚ₃ points; 468 further local certificates at thirteen primes.

Scope. The upstream descent remains conditional; local points do not establish global rational points.

Rational enclosures for a compact-window Weil-form estimate

Reconstruction of trace, variance, prime and pole-tail bounds for L=0.81 and T=596.
