Evidence and verification scope

Preserved sources

The public derivatives retain scientific arguments, failed approaches, open steps, scripts and recorded outputs. The manifest identifies the source records, extracted artifacts, published hashes and editorial changes.

An inventory and its hashes establish what was transferred. They do not certify every historical claim.

Selected exact rechecks

The dedicated standard-library verifier checks 10 selected norm determinants in supplied integer multiplication tables of dimension 21, and 882 table-composition identities. It also checks ranks, filtrations, weights and covariance of recorded vectors over F₇ for q = 683, 907.

Verifier sourceExact replay output

The current verification commands use standard libraries. Historical scripts may require mpmath, sympy, numpy or scipy, local companion modules and adaptation of their original execution paths. Archiving those scripts does not make every historical workflow independently runnable.

The selected replay does not reconstruct local embeddings, the precision-6/7 comparison or the coefficients e_loc and e8. Identification and maximality of number-field orders, global unit groups, class-group and reciprocity claims, and the Riemann hypothesis remain outside its scope. An older PASS label is a historical record, not evidence that every calculation was rerun during this import.

Coverage limits and citation

Transfer is complete for the inventoried collection of 151 supplied scientific source files. Each source is represented by a public narrative, standalone file or extracted bundle inventory.

This closed inventory does not certify completeness of every private historical research source outside the collection.
