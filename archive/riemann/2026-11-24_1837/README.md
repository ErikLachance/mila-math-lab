## 1. Integral finite trace generation, without assuming the specialized coefficient ring is flat
Let O be a complete DVR with finite residue field k of characteristic p>3, fraction field F. Let S be a complete Noetherian local O-algebra with finite residue field, and rho:G->GL3(S) continuous with topologically finitely generated image. The previous checkpoint gave the arithmetic finite-generation argument conditional on its ramification assumptions. Fix d topological generators, and include their inverses as additional matrices (m=2d). Let T_w be the O-algebra of traces of all finite words in these matrices.
Claim: T_w is finitely generated as an O-algebra even when S has O-torsion. Proof is by a universal polynomial identity, not by reducing specialized S modulo p. Use P=O[x_ij^(h)], generic m-tuples of 3x3 matrices, graded by total degree. Let R=P^{GL3} be the group-scheme invariants; coaction target P tensor O[GL3] is O-torsion-free. For every degree j, R_j is a finite O-module, and R_j/pi R_j injects into P_j/pi P_j: if invariant f=pi g, cancel pi in the coaction equality to see g invariant. Its image consists of invariants over k. Over algebraic closure of k, Donkin's invariant generation theorem and Newton identities (3! invertible) give generation by word traces, and Derksen–Makam gives homogeneous generator degree <= (m+1)*3^4. This implies generation by the traces of words of length <= D=81(m+1): all homogeneous characteristic-coefficient generators needed in degrees <=D can be expressed as traces of powers of words of total degree <=D, via Newton. Descent of the expression to k is linear algebra: for each fixed degree, span of finitely many monomials in short trace functions commutes with extension of the field. Thus every invariant in R_j reduces to a linear combination of reductions of such trace monomials of degree j. Let U be O-algebra generated by short traces in P. Then R_j=U_j+pi R_j, by saturation of R_j in P_j. Since R_j/U_j is a finite O-module, Nakayama gives R_j=U_j. This holds degree by degree, no infinite formal sum. Consequently every universal word trace is a polynomial over O in the finitely many short traces, and evaluation in arbitrary S preserves the polynomial identities. No exact invariant base-change theorem for specialized S required; no claim all invariants of S are traces. The bound is very large and depends on d; do not confuse it with a uniform bound on the number of Galois generators across primes. For p=2,3 the reduction to traces is not valid generally (see tests); use characteristic coefficients instead.
Sources: Donkin, Invariants of several matrices, Invent. Math. 110 (1992), 389–401, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01231338 (bibliographic entry consulted, not full text); Derksen–Makam https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06617, full HTML introduction Theorem 1.4 and Corollary 1.6; https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DV Nakayama. The integral lifting argument above is supplied here, not attributed to these sources; no novelty claim.

## 2. Necessary and sufficient vertical criterion for FinA, conditional on finitely many local trace systems
Assume finitely many complete characteristic-zero local trace systems occur for S and that S[1/pi] is Jacobson with maximal residues finite extensions of F corresponding to local points (the standard formal generic-fibre fact used in the previous checkpoint). Let T=O[tr rho(g):g in G]. For each vertical minimal prime P of S (pi in P), impose condition V: every trace of a word of length <=D in the selected generators and inverses becomes algebraic over k in S/P. Equivalent to being a constant in the residual field when S has residue k: in a local domain with residue k, a nonzero algebraic element over k generates a field that embeds into the residue field. If residue is a finite extension of k, algebraicity still suffices.
Then T is a finite O-module if and only if V holds (assuming finite local trace systems for sufficiency). Necessity: finite algebra implies all elements integral over O, and reduction at vertical prime gives algebraicity. For sufficiency fix a short trace t. Its values at all local points lie in finite coefficient extensions, are integral, and take finitely many values. Take monic polynomial f_t in O[X] whose roots include these values and their F-conjugates (product of minimal polynomials). If no local points, take 1. Then f_t(t) belongs to all maximal ideals of S[1/pi], hence to its nilradical. At any horizontal minimal prime P (pi not in P), f_t(t)=0 in (S/P)[1/pi] and thus in S/P. For each vertical minimal prime, V supplies a monic polynomial over k annihilating t there. Lift those finitely many polynomials to monic O-polynomials and multiply them with f_t. Result h_t(t) is in every minimal prime of S. S Noetherian => its nilradical is nilpotent, say exponent e. Thus h_t(X)^e is monic and kills t in S, even when S has torsion or generic nilpotents. By section 1 there are finitely many short generators, each integral, so T_w finite over O. It is compact because O is compact; continuous image of a finite product of O is compact and hence closed in Hausdorff S. Every group trace is a limit of finite-word traces and therefore T=T_w. Prior 1717 checkpoint's normalized projectors and weight-cycle reduction then give FinA provided that normalization and the necessary identification of the invariant entry algebra are valid for the actual S. This removes the need to prove flatness of the entire global S; only the concrete vertical trace condition is required. It is NOT a proof of V for the global deformation ring, nor of automorphy/finite local trace systems. No assumption of S reduced. If S is flat, V is vacuous and this recovers prior conditional result. Unlike the previous finite-generic-rank argument, this handles vertical components with arbitrarily many off-diagonal parameters as long as their traces are constant/algebraic.
Potential way to establish V: classify residual-characteristic semisimplifications of rho in Frac(S/P) for vertical P, using exact inertia conditions and global ramification, not just residue-field specialization. It suffices that all such generic vertical semisimplifications have traces algebraic over k. Global mod-p deformation families can have variable traces, so this must be proved, not assumed. Counterexample to automatic V remains S=O[[x]]/(pi*x), rho(g)=diag((1+x)^g,1,(1+x)^(-g)) for G=Z_p. Its vertical trace at 1 is 3+x^2/(1+x), transcendental over k, despite unique char-zero trace. Nilpotent vertical deviations alone are harmless by the polynomial power argument. No actual vertical prime of the target global S has been analyzed this run.

## 3. Exact torsion saturation invariance for ALG.0060's particular cotangent construction
Let A be the Z-graded O-algebra of ALG.0060, x:A->O the base point, m=ker x, Q=saturation_O(m^2), U=A[pi^infty]. U is a homogeneous ideal (finite homogeneous decomposition), U subset m since O torsion-free, and U subset Q. Set A'=A/U, x'=x descended, m'=m/U. The preimage of Q'=saturation_O((m')^2) equals Q: if pi^n a in m^2+U, then for some k multiplication by pi^k kills the U term, so pi^(n+k)a in m^2. Reverse inclusion immediate. Therefore A/Q ~= A'/Q' as graded O-algebras and C_j identical for all j. Quotient by images of any homogeneous F_i gives exactly the same O-module and length. All char-zero points, torus orbits, formal-coordinate maps to F[[T]] and their field-valued derivations are unchanged by killing U: torsion maps to zero, and derivations into a char-zero vector space kill U by the Leibniz rule on pi^n u. Hence the formal-coordinate hypothesis, point exclusions, and rigidity conditions 2–4 of ALG.0060 are preserved under this operation. If A0 is complete Noetherian local O-algebra then A'_0=A0/U0 is likewise (U0 ideal closed by Noetherian completeness); homogeneous generation preserved. Condition 1 remains an independent requirement on the image of A'_0 in A'/I': if original condition 1 holds it descends, but it need not be proved for original A if it can be proved for A'. This is a legitimate way to apply ALG.0060 to its torsion-free replacement while retaining the target *saturated* cotangent length. It does NOT establish that this quotient automatically satisfies the finite image condition; quotient by I' can reintroduce torsion. Nor does it show the integral derivation lifting in AGR.1166 is preserved. Indeed raw derivations into O/pi^n can change: A=O[x]/(pi^a x,x^2), x maps to zero; D(x) can be any element annihilated by pi^a, while A/U=O has only zero derivation. Do not replace integral tangent spaces by rational ones. Also do not quotient by nilradical: O[x]/(x^2) has saturated cotangent rank1 but its reduced quotient O has rank0. This is why horizontal torsion saturation is permissible here and generic reduction is not. ## 4. A precise degree-two orbit-slice determinant; missing comparison to cotangent lattice
Under conditions of ALG.0060, set B=(A/I)[1/pi]. Finite generation of image of A0 over O as a module (interpretation required here) implies B is finite-type over F, and a in image A0 is integral over O. Use finitely many homogeneous nonzero-weight generators. For each mixed point choose first nonzero negative generator u and first nonzero positive generator v in fixed lists, with u weight -d, d=1 or2. Define disjoint locally closed slices by setting earlier generators in each list to zero, v invertible, and u=1. Every orbit in that stratum meets its slice: solve t^d=u(point). Stabilizer size h=gcd(abs(weights of all nonzero generators)) divides d. Distinct slice points in each orbit number exactly d/h, hence 1 or2. At each mixed point all tangent vectors of B are multiples of torus tangent by condition 3; torus tangent nonzero since char0 and u nonzero, and sends u to -d*u. Imposing u=1 therefore annihilates tangent space, even after imposing the earlier zero conditions or inverting v. Every geometric point of each finite-type slice has zero tangent space, so the slice is zero-dimensional and reduced over algebraic closure (local ring at point has maximal ideal with m/m^2=0, Nakayama). It is a finite reduced F-algebra, product of finite separable fields (possibly zero), since F perfect. Consequently all points and slice values of a are algebraic over F. Let E be product of these slice algebras, a_E induced image. Since condition 4 makes a_E invertible in E, and a integral, E has finite free O-order O_E = product of rings of integers of its field factors, stable under multiplication by a_E. Smith normal form gives exact length length_O(O_E/a_E O_E)=ord_O(det_F multiplication by a_E)=sum over all geometric slice points v(a). By the orbit multiplicity bound and nonnegative v(a) this is <=2 sum over mixed orbits v(a). This supplies a finite integral determinant with the same numerical right side as ALG.0060. However the crucial map from C_-1/<F_i> into (or quotient/subquotient of) O_E/a_E O_E has NOT been constructed. A determinant bound on this unrelated lattice alone is not ALG.0060. Normalizing u=1 uses potentially nonintegral torus scalars; no assumption that normalized matrix entries lie in O_E, only a does (finite A0 image). Condition 2 and formal-coordinate conditions have not yet been exploited in this determinant calculation; likely precisely relevant to the missing cotangent comparison. Need study integral equations before division by pi or a. If catalogue's 'finitely generated over O' means algebra generation rather than module finiteness, integrality of a requires additional proof; the slice argument for finite type still holds, but determinant inequality may not. No new proof of full factor 2 theorem.

## 5. Crystalline tangent route: precise conditional factorization and obstruction
For a local universal ring R and fixed potentially crystalline Hodge and inertial type, Kisin's characteristic-zero deformation locus gives a quotient R_cris with the relevant period property. Suppose a local component quotient R/J is reduced after inverting pi and all of its finite-extension points have this fixed type. By Jacobson density the ideal defining R_cris maps to zero in (R/J)[1/pi]; hence every characteristic-zero tangent there factors through the crystalline quotient. If R/J is pi-torsion-free, the ideal already maps to zero integrally. Merely knowing a collection of points is crystalline on a nonreduced ring is insufficient (nilpotent directions). Must also use the actual Artinian-coefficient deformation interpretation to identify tangent class in H^1_f after restriction to an extension where the representation is crystalline; fixed potentially crystalline type and descent require verification, not assumed. Prior D096 local selected-prime intersection suggests a reduced horizontal quotient, but D096 and D097 catalogue URLs returned inaccessible this run, so exact fixed-type condition and factorization have NOT been verified.
