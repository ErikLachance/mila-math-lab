## B. Rank-two coefficient-place monodromy obstruction and resolution under classical comparison

D051 gives diagonal inertia characters at ell epsilon_ell^{e}chi_1 and epsilon_ell^{-e}chi_2. Rank-one diagonal characters again are finite times cyclotomic powers times continuous unramified characters; the latter's Frobenius value has ell-adic absolute value 1 because the image of the compact group is compact. Therefore WD Frobenius eigenvalues have ell-adic slopes +e and -e (possibly both signs reversed by convention), by exactness.
