## 1. Exact full inertia flag from the actual local product equations
Notation from D096: H=ker eta_l intersect ker tau, I_H=H intersect I_l, tau=psi_l restricted locally, psi_1=Psi_l, psi_2=1, psi_3=Psi_l^-1. On I_H these are eps^e,1,eps^-e. The fixed s0 in I_H has eps(s0) of infinite order, so the three eigenvalues of A=r(s0) are pairwise distinct over any characteristic-zero field. In its ordered eigenbasis put N_i(s)=r(s)-psi_i(s) I. The source imposes N_1(s1)N_2(s2)N_3(s3)=0 for every independent triple in I_H, not just characteristic polynomial equality.
Take s2=s3=s0. N_2(s0)N_3(s0)=c_1 P_1, where P_1 is projector onto first eigenspace and c_1 nonzero. Hence N_1(s)P_1=0: the first line is invariant with char psi_1. Take s1=s2=s0. N_1(s0)N_2(s0)=c_3 P_3, hence P_3 N_3(s)=0: the plane ker P_3 is invariant and quotient character psi_3. The remaining diagonal character equals psi_2 by trace (or characteristic polynomial). Thus r(I_H) is upper triangular in this fixed ordered basis. Conversely, for any such ordered triangular family the triple product vanishes: N_3 maps into first two coordinates, N_2 maps this plane into first line, N_1 kills that line.
Uniqueness: any I_H-line affording psi_1 must be in first eigenspace of A; any I_H-plane with quotient char psi_3 must be ker P_3 because the corresponding dual eigenline at s0 is unique. If a genuine representation of a larger group normalizes I_H and preserves these characters under conjugation, it preserves both subspaces, hence has the same full flag. For the matrix part of the semidirect representation this applies to elements with eta=1, not indiscriminately to eta=-1. It applies to the genuine two-dimensional corner extension W on the full local decomposition group once W is constructed.
Negative control: matrices diag(2,1,1/2) and a lower-triangular matrix with diagonal (3,1,1/3), (2,1) entry 1, satisfy the individual polynomial conditions and have a different invariant direction; the triple relation fails. Individual characteristic polynomials alone cannot force this ordered flag.

## 2. Component-selection argument: exact full inertia characters (not merely congruences)
This addresses the subtle case where residual quadratic twists can be indistinguishable. For each selected prime P in the definition of I_loc, put R=Rloc/P, a domain, with the base representation specialization x0:R->O (P contained in its kernel), so ell is nonzero in R. Work in Frac(R). By section 1, the I_H flag is unique. Since eta_l is trivial on I_l and I_H is normal in I_l, the flag is stable under I_l. Its ordered characters k_i on I_l extend eps^e,1,eps^-e. The ratios k_1/eps^e, k_2, k_3/eps^-e factor through I_l/I_H, a group of order at most 2, because psi_l^2=1. Consequently these ratios are signs in the fraction field.
For s in I_l, k_i(s)=tr(P_i r(s)) with P_i=product_{j!=i}(A-psi_j(s0)I)/c_i. This expression belongs to R[1/ell], since c_i are nonzero constants in O. Evaluate using x0:R[1/ell]->Frac(O): all denominators remain nonzero, projectors select the same ordered diagonal characters at the base point. The signs therefore equal tau(s),1,tau(s) respectively (tau^-1=tau). This holds on every selected component P; multiply out the characteristic polynomial coefficients to avoid denominators and use R domain to conclude the identity lies in P itself. Intersect all selected primes, then push to S. Thus the exact characteristic polynomial for full inertia I_l is (X-Psi_l(s))(X-1)(X-Psi_l(s)^-1), not just its restriction to I_H. At other places above ell, conjugacy in G_Q and polarization give the same multiset (inversion leaves it invariant). For local points, this also gives the ordered characters on the local kernel-of-eta decomposition group. This reasoning assumes D096 catalogue accurately describes the Lean definition; independent mathematical derivation, not raw code certification.
Why the component argument matters: at ell=5, e=1, for u in F_5^*, quadratic q(u)=u^2, the traces q(u)(u+u^-1) and u+u^-1 coincide mod 5 for all u. At u=2 their rational lifts differ by sign and are nonzero. More generally the residual ambiguity occurs precisely when 2e=(ell-1)/2 mod (ell-1): if g is primitive, q(g)=-1, equality forces g^(2e)=-1; conversely all nonsquare powers then satisfy that relation. Thus do not infer the exact sign from residual trace congruences alone.

## 3. Classification of local-point reducible representations and GC.1118 conditions 6-9
Let rho=matrix part restricted to G_K at a local point. Residual representation is the direct sum of the three distinct global characters Psi_bar,1,Psi_bar^-1 (marked g1 distinguishes them). Local point yields integral lattice: f(S) lies in a finite coefficient extension, as proved in section 5 below. On G_K polarization gives rho^c isomorphic to rho^vee. Standard reduction of stable saturated sublattices and quotient lattices identifies residual semisimplification of the constituents with the original three distinct characters.
If rho has three one-dimensional composition factors, at every inertia above ell their multiset of restrictions is exactly Psi,1,Psi^-1 by section 2 and independence of distinct characters over characteristic zero. Residual restrictions to full inertia are pairwise distinct: eps mod ell surjective there, Psi_bar^2=eps_bar^(2e) since tau^2=1, and ell-1 does not divide 2e. Thus the matching is forced by the residual global label. Away from ell the representation factors on inertia through its residual image (D096 property 2), a finite group of order prime to ell on G_K, and reduction is injective on the corresponding roots of unity. Each character divided by its prescribed Psi-power is therefore unramified everywhere and residual-trivial. Since ell does not divide h_K, class field theory makes that ratio trivial. Hence trace system equals the base trace even for nontrivial extensions.
If composition dimensions are 2+1, polarization preserves the sole one-dimensional constituent. Its residual label must be fixed by conjugate dual, which exchanges Psi_bar and Psi_bar^-1, leaving only 1. The same inertia and class-number argument makes the character trivial. Determinant rho is also trivial (residual 1, full inertia determinant 1, prime-to-ell inertia quotient away from ell). Polarization then preserves the two-dimensional constituent U. The two distinct composition factors must split: if U is a submodule, polarization gives a quotient isomorphic to U, and the composite U->rho->U is nonzero since its kernel would otherwise be contained in a one-dimensional space. If U is initially a quotient, reverse by polarization. Thus rho=U direct_sum 1. The marked diagonal B=rho(g1)-rho(c g1^-1 c)=diag(d,0,-d) preserves both constituents, and B is zero on the trivial line; since d!=0 the trivial line is exactly e2. Hence U is the (e1,e3) corner and rho actually satisfies type II in normalized coordinates. All remaining cases are type I (irreducible). This classifies local points, not arbitrary nonlocal entry-algebra maps.
For type II use the prior construction W on G_Q with W|G_K=U and W(c)=+/-D, D=diag(1,-1), det W=eta. Section 1 applied to the corner gives the unique I_H-line with char eps^e and the other diagonal eps^-e. The middle 1 of rho is a separate coordinate; the distinct s0 eigenspaces ensure the first and third eigenlines lie in the corner. Since W is a genuine representation, the I_H-line remains stable on the full local decomposition group. By section 2 its ordered inertia characters are eps^e tau, eps^-e tau. The quadratic local tau factors on inertia through the action on ell-th roots of unity, since the ell-power part of (Z/ell^n)^* is a finite ell-group and tau has order <=2. This establishes the flag requirement of GC.1118 condition 8, and condition 6 follows from restricting to ker tau (e.g. fixing N-th roots). The inertia trace part of condition 7 follows exactly. Choose W(c)=-D to give the residual congruence outside G_K: for g=hc, residual trace is -Psi(h)+Psi(h)^-1 = eta(g)Psi(g)+Psi(g)^-1, since Psi(c)=1. For g in G_K this congruence follows directly from rho. Both extensions have det eta and trace c=0. These deductions are conditional on the prior W construction and exact source-model hypotheses, not proof of GC.1118 vanishing.
Condition 9 proof (no automorphy needed for this step). Let G=G_K, N0=ker(eps|G), I an inertia group above ell inside G. eps(I)=Z_ell^* because the full cyclotomic tower is totally ramified locally and ell unramified in K; hence G=N0 I. Suppose U|N0 reducible. If it has precisely one invariant line, normality makes that line G-invariant. If it has >=3, every N0 matrix is scalar, and a line stable under I exists by the local flag, hence is G-invariant. Both contradict irreducibility of U. If it has exactly two invariant lines, G permutes them, nontrivially by irreducibility. This permutation character is trivial on N0 and must be nontrivial on I since G=N0 I. Choose s in I swapping lines; U(s) has trace 0, and so does U(s t^(2n)) for every integer n, with t in I_H such that eps(t) infinite order (the fixed s0 suffices), since even powers preserve both lines. But section 2 gives trace =tau(s)*(eps(s)^e eps(t)^(2en) + eps(s)^-e eps(t)^(-2en)), which can vanish for at most one integer n (otherwise eps(t) has finite order). Contradiction. Thus U|N0 irreducible. This closes the prior specific condition 9 gap under source-model assumptions. ## 4. H9 FinA: conditional module-finiteness reduction, including nilpotents
Separated-module lemma. Let O be a complete DVR with uniformizer pi, K0=Frac(O); let M be an O-module with intersection pi^n M=0. If M/pi M has finite k-dimension d, then M is generated over O by any d lifts m_i of a basis. For x in M, repeatedly choose coefficients modulo pi and express the residual as pi*x_1, giving x-sum_i a_(i,n)m_i in pi^n M. The coefficients a_(i,n) converge in O. For every n, replacing with their limits preserves this membership; separatedness gives equality. No completeness of M required. If M torsion-free of finite K0-rank r, then dim(M/pi M)<=r: a finite set independent mod pi is K0-independent by clearing denominators and normalizing a coefficient to a unit. So a separated torsion-free finite-rank submodule is finite free. Any O-submodule of a complete Noetherian local S as in D096 is separated (pi^n M subset m_S^n S). Finite module is compact in S, hence closed in its Hausdorff m-adic topology.
Application to actual trace algebra T=O[tr rho(g):g in G_K] subset S. The universal image of G_K in GL3(S) is topologically finitely generated: the universal inertia condition kills inertia outside the finite set of primes over ell*N*disc K. The residual kernel in the image is pro-ell, by filtration of 1+m_S M3(S) with finite elementary abelian ell quotients. It is a quotient of the maximal pro-ell quotient of the Galois group of a finite number field cut out by the residual representation, unramified outside finitely many primes. Hermite-Minkowski gives finitely many degree-ell extensions of that field unramified there, so H^1 with F_ell is finite; finite Burnside basis theorem on all finite quotients gives finite topological generation. Add finitely many lifts of the residual image generators. This does not assume the whole unramified-outside-S Galois group is finitely generated.
Let T_w be O-algebra generated by traces of words in these finite group generators and their inverses. They are actual group traces. In S[1/pi], B=T_w[1/pi] (or its image) is a finite-type K0-algebra by the characteristic-zero Procesi-Razmyslov finite-generation theorem for traces of products of finitely many matrices, applied also to inverse matrices as additional generators. Assume finitely many complete trace systems occur at local points of S. Standard generic-fibre Jacobson theorem for complete noetherian local O-algebras identifies all maximal-ideal residue embeddings of S[1/pi] with such local points (finite coefficient extensions). Then the kernel of the evaluation B -> product_{j=1}^m L_j over representatives of these trace systems is exactly its nilradical: element vanishing there vanishes at every maximal ideal of S[1/pi], hence is nilpotent, also in B as a subalgebra; converse clear. B_red injects into a finite-dimensional K0-vector space. B itself is finite dimensional because B is Noetherian, its nilradical nilpotent and each successive nilradical quotient finitely generated over B_red. This controls generic nilpotents without assuming S reduced.
If S is O-torsion-free, T_w injects into B; the separated-module lemma makes it finite over O and hence closed in S. Every trace of every g is the m-adic limit of traces of words in the generators, so T=T_w, finite over O. By the previous checkpoint's projector/weight-cycle proof and henselian argument, S0=T and FinA follows. More generally one may replace S-flatness by proving finite O-length of the torsion submodule of T_w (then use separatedness of image in S/S[pi^infty] or exact sequence after proving it). Finite trace systems should follow from section 3 classification plus H9 automorphy parts 3-4, H8 level/comparison, uniform conductor/weight bounds, finite Hecke systems and Chebotarev. These inputs have not all been independently discharged. In particular, H9 automorphy for all algebraic (not just local) points is still separate; no circular claim that FinA has already been proved for the global S.
Concrete obstruction to dropping flatness: S=O[[x]]/(pi*x) is complete Noetherian local, generic fibre K0, only one local char-zero point. The continuous Z_ell representation diag((1+x)^g,1,(1+x)^(-g)) exists via binomial series (residual identity). Its trace at g=1 minus 3 is y=x^2/(1+x); pi*y=0 and powers y^n have distinct lowest degrees 2n in k[[x]], hence are linearly independent mod pi and give infinite O-module rank in the sense of infinite required generators (all torsion, not infinite K0-rank). Thus finite generic trace systems alone do NOT imply integral FinA even for a finitely generated pro-ell group and a continuous representation. This is a toy countermodel, not a counterexample to D103's full arithmetic hypotheses.
A second obstruction: T=O+pi*x*O[x] inside S=O[[x]] is torsion-free, has constant image mod pi*S, but T/pi*T contains independent classes pi*x^n for all n>=1; confusing T intersect pi*S with pi*T is invalid. Either actual finite generic dimension plus torsion-free or a genuine internal finite T/pi*T proof is needed.

## 5. Partial tangent-to-Selmer bridge (away from ell, continuity)
Let f:S->Qbar_ell be a local O-point of the complete Noetherian local ring S. All f(S) are integral: elements of m go to positive valuation and any unit differs from a lift in O^* by an element of m (residue fields same). Choose finitely many generators of m. The minimum of their positive valuations (ignoring zeros) gives uniform linear decay of f(m^n). By completeness, f(s) is limit of O-polynomials in the finitely many chosen generator images; the finite extension L of Frac(O) generated by them is complete, so f(S) lies in O_L. It is finite O-module as submodule of O_L. If all generator images vanish the claim is immediate.
For any algebraic O-linear derivation D:S->Qbar_ell at f, the ideal ker f is finitely generated since S Noetherian. Choose finite O-module generators of f(S) and lifts to S. Each s is a combination of those lifts with coefficients in O plus an element of ker f, which is a combination of fixed ideal generators with coefficients in S. By the Leibniz rule, D(s) is a sum of finite many fixed values D(lifts), D(ideal generators) times integral coefficients (since f(kernel)=0). Thus D(S) is uniformly valuation bounded; applying Leibniz on products of m-generators shows D(m^n) decays at least as (n-1) times the minimum positive valuation, up to fixed constant; if all images zero then D(m^2)=0. D is continuous. An algebraic tangent on the true S at a local point therefore gives a continuous adjoint cocycle via differential of the universal representation.
For p!=ell, source D096 kills kernel of residual representation on inertia, so differentiated cocycle vanishes there and factors through the finite residual inertia image H0. Over characteristic zero any 1-cocycle a on H0 is a coboundary: summing a(sh)=a(s)+s a(h) over h gives a(s)=s*x-x for x=-(sum_h a(h))/|H0|. Thus actual S-tangent cocycles satisfy the away-from-ell Selmer condition of D097. This does NOT establish the crystalline-period condition at ell (B_cris), nor automatic extension of arbitrary derivations of the graded model or entry algebra to S; source AGR.1166 explicitly needs tangent lifting. Do not mistake local triangularity alone for crystallinity. The product-equation flag also expresses d_a=c_a*c_(a+1)*(k_a(phi)-eps(phi)*k_(a+1)(phi)), by tr(P_a r(phi)); saturation by delta removes components where delta vanishes identically, NOT all specializations with delta=0. In particular, generic nonresonance must not be transported to every point by mere specialization.
