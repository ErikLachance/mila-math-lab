## A. Sharp positive-semidefinite restriction inequality, improving the preceding trace bound
Let Q,R be finite Hermitian matrices of size D>0, 0<=R<=Q in Loewner order, with tr Q=T>0 and tr R=rT>0, r in (0,1], q=1-r. Set eta=D tr(Q^2)/T^2-1 and v=D tr(R^2)/(rT)^2-1. Both nonnegative by Cauchy-Schwarz. The following inequality requires NO commutation and NO block dimension constraints:

    r v <= q + sqrt(eta v).
    v <= [(sqrt(eta)+sqrt(eta+4rq))/(2r)]^2 =: B(eta,q).   (A1)

Proof: P=Q-R is positive semidefinite, hence tr(RP)>=0 (trace of R^(1/2) P R^(1/2)). Therefore tr(R^2)<=tr(RQ). Center Q and R by their scalar trace means. The cross term is at most sqrt(tr[(Q-TI/D)^2] tr[(R-rTI/D)^2]) by real Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz. Thus tr(R^2)<=rT^2/D*(1+sqrt(eta v)). Divide by rT^2/D and rearrange r(1+v)<=1+sqrt(eta v). Solve the quadratic inequality for sqrt(v). The bound is also valid for v=0 or eta=0. A rational envelope by Young is v<=2q/r+eta/r^2, since sqrt(eta v)<=r v/2+eta/(2r).

The previous generic bound using only tr(R^2)<=tr(Q^2) was V=(1+eta)/r^2-1. A1 never worse, strictly better whenever q>0: rV-q=(eta+q)/r>=0 and (rV-q)^2-eta V=(1+eta)q^2/r^2. This is a direct exact comparison without numerical subtraction. In the ideal case eta=0, A1 becomes v<=q/(1-q), whereas V=(1-q)^-2-1. For eta=0, equality occurs by deleting a subspace from a scalar Q. In particular for q=1/4 the new normalized defect bound is 1/3 rather than 7/9.

Sharpness for nonzero eta too: take an orthogonal decomposition of dimensions k and D-k, 0<k<D, define R=(rT/k)I on the first and zero on the second, P=(qT/(D-k))I on the second and zero on the first. Then v=(D-k)/k, eta=(D/k)r^2+(D/(D-k))q^2-1. If r>=k/D, then r v-q>=0 and (r v-q)^2=eta v, so A1 is an equality. No universal strictly smaller bound at these parameter triples using only q,eta and PSD ordering. These are matrix examples, NOT examples of the period maps in the RH proof. Tests also include noncommuting matrices.

## B. Apply A1 to ALG.1746 and combine with signed slope

Let a=mean(w_j)>0. Relative variance <= [e-(a-1)^2]/a^2 <=e/(1-e), by ( (1-e)-a )^2>=0 after rearranging. Covariance of w_j/a with indicator of discarded coordinates gives

    |q-F| <= sqrt(e F(1-F)/(1-e)).   (B1)

For 0<=F<=alpha<=1/4 the upper RHS F+sqrt(e F(1-F)/(1-e)) is nondecreasing in F, so q<=alpha+sqrt(e alpha(1-alpha)/(1-e)). With e<=sigma^2/4 and sigma<=1/20, q<1 (indeed <.263). At a fixed t>=0 the left side decreases with q and eta. Use the upper q and eta bounds in A1 if actual values unavailable.

Here we retain the previously discarded diagonal-exclusion correction; proof expands tr(Q^2) as sum squared pair HS norms, uses triangle inequality per diagonal and lower trace R_d(1-sqrt(e0)). Counting size condition (ANT.0103) requires (bar eta+4 sqrt(bar e)) x^c_* b <= K x^eps |T| a with a=xb. It is sufficient to have bar eta+4 sqrt(bar e)=O(x^-h) for h>max(0,c_*-1) and K>0, using |T|>=1, x>=1; constants fixed before x. The linear diagonal substitution of the last checkpoint gives the weaker 4bar e/3 term too. This addresses whether linear restriction can help at polynomial tolerance, but raw diagonal moments already exponentially small in the actual construction, so loosening them alone cannot be claimed to reduce Hermitian rank k or lift count m. A distinct necessary rank obstruction: any family with eta_full moment defect has rank(Q)>=D/(1+eta_full), by Cauchy-Schwarz over its nonzero eigenvalues. Rank(Q)<=sum d_z. ## D. Alternative aggregate raw diagonal moment condition (conditional)
From raw second-moment hypotheses let xi=exp(-kf log k), A_z=K_zz-R_zz-I, B_z=R_zz. In the direct sum of HS spaces, sum ||A_z||^2<=Gamma sum d_z^2/D_* and sum ||B_z||^2<=xi R_d. Minkowski gives aggregate e<= (sqrt(Gamma sum d_z^2/(D_* R_d))+sqrt(xi))^2. This uses the dimension-weighted mean of d_z rather than a uniform maximum. The full moment estimate of B remains valid with aggregate e replacing e0: sum ||K_zz||^2<=R_d(1+sqrt(e))^2 by direct-sum triangle. Thus the algebraic stage needs only this weighted mean and total source rank, not identical per-source dimensions. If raw conditions can be proved for enlarged comparable source dimensions d_z/D_* about x^-h/Gamma, then e=O(x^-h) and R_d/D_*>=x^h would need source count of order Gamma x^(2h), instead of Gamma^2 x^C_H. Important adversarial correction: enlarged e=O(x^-h) alone does NOT imply eta_full=O(x^-h); the trace denominator in B can incur O(sqrt(e)) loss. The counting surrogate improves the explicit diagonal term, not this hidden trace-normalization loss. That still saves one exponential factor Gamma compared with the original selection, conditionally.
