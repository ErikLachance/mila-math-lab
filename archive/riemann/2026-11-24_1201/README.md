## A. Exhaustion of character cases in GC.0079
Fix primitive base character psi, order o, conductor Npsi; q,d,nu,t,N,E and Bpsi as in GC.0079. Work over a finite DVR O containing the values of all characters of Gal(E/Q); this can be arranged by finite free extension without changing normalized lengths (continuous cochains commute with finite free scalar extension). Write C for the even character group of Q(mu_{2^{nu+2}})^+, and chi_q,chi_d for the two quadratic characters of conductor q,d. Every character phi of E is a product theta psi^a chi_q^b chi_d^c, theta in C, b,c in {0,1}. This follows by taking the annihilator of the intersection of the four subgroups in the full finite abelian character group. Equivalently characters on a subgroup of a finite abelian group extend to the full group over the algebraic closure. Representation need not be unique when Kpsi intersects the real cyclotomic field: use distinct characters, not representations. Number of characters n=[E:Q]<=4*o*2^nu<=4N (also n<=phi(N)<=N).

The odd part of ord(phi) divides the odd part of o. At q or d, the local factor has order at most two, since both primes are coprime to Npsi. At 2 the local factor has 2-power order. For any odd ell outside Bpsi, ell does not divide o or Npsi, so phi has order prime to ell. If ell=q or d, then ell-1>2(t-1) and ell-1 cannot divide 2r for 1<=r<t. For other ell outside Bpsi the ell-local component is trivial. Thus the following partition (choose first applicable branch) is exhaustive:
1. Good ell and ell-1 not dividing 2r: I, all phi.
2. Otherwise r>=2 and ord(phi*) even: II.
3. Otherwise good ell: ell-1 divides 2r; hence r>=2 because 3 is excluded. Neither q nor d can equal ell, so phi*=phi, of odd order. Its trivial member is Z, other members III.
4. Bad ell and r=1: V if the specified R1<=2^{c2(phi)}; else low-conductor fallback C below.
5. Bad ell and r>=2 not covered by II: ord(phi*) odd. Taking prime-to-ell part cannot remove any 2-primary character component because ell is odd. Removing the ell-local component does not touch the 2,q,d factors (ell distinct from all three). Therefore all 2,q,d factors are trivial, including potential cancellation at 2. Consequently conductor(phi)|Npsi and phi is a power of psi (use CRT on the factors other than 2,q,d; the possibly canceled 2-part cannot increase conductor). There are at most o possible phi for all r and all varying nu,q,d. Fixed tower fallback B below.

## B. Fixed-tower transfer: coefficient of B independent of t and nu
For a fixed bad ell let L'=Q(mu_{ell*Npsi}) with the prescribed ell-cyclotomic layer adjoined. Its degree D and number k of generators of its finite abelian Galois group are fixed by psi and j(ell). Each residual branch-5 character factors through L'. Restrict its strict local H1 group V to L'. The target is contained in S_{ell,r}(L') tensor O: outside ell local vanishing implies unramifiedness, and at ell vanishing is imposed. Strict local vanishing at ALL primes holds for V, because outside ell N unramified local cohomology is A/(Frob−1)A=0 for divisible A=Frac(O)/O and Frobenius scalar ell-independent p^{-r} times a root of unity, unequal to 1 when r>0. Over L' phi is trivial. Global inflation-restriction bounds the kernel by H1(Gal(L'/Q), A^{G_L'}). Invariants A^{G_L'} are finite cyclic over O, since some cyclotomic scalar to the power -r is not 1 (infinite cyclotomic image). A cocycle is determined by its values on k generators, so H1 is a subquotient of (A^{G_L'})^k, hence generated by at most k over the DVR, and killed by D. Thus normalized length <=k*v_ell(D). This argument works even if the ell-Sylow is noncyclic, unlike the sharper cyclic estimate in prior checkpoint for E itself.
For each r and phi, v_{ell,r,phi} <= length_Zell S_{ell,r}(L')+k*v_ell(D). Summing over the at-most-o fixed chars, then all r<t, gives <=o B+o(t-1)k*v_ell(D). Multiplying log ell and summing finitely many bad primes gives C_psi B+O_psi,j(t). In particular no spurious tB or nB factors; the constants may depend on the fixed j. If requiring C independent of j, the correction can alternatively use the intermediate Q(mu_{ell Npsi}) restriction kernel and monotonicity from that fixed base to L'; the kernel for the ell-cyclotomic extension vanishes by the earlier procyclic descent, but this sharper uniform-in-j detail has not been fully audited here. The source statement chooses C after psi and j, sufficient for this derivation.

## C. Low 2-conductor, r=1: polynomial via fixed degree H2
For bad ell, phi1 is the prime-to-ell part of the product of odd local factors. Its order divides M_ell=lcm(o_{ell'},2), independent of nu,q,d. Therefore f0=ord_{lcm(4,ord phi1)} ell divides F_ell=ord_{lcm(4,M_ell)} ell (both defined since ell coprime to modulus), and n0<=v2(ell^{F_ell}-1). This proves R1<=Rmax_ell=16o(ell Npsi)^2*4^{v2(ell^{F_ell}-1)} fixed. If V fails at r=1, 2^{c2}<Rmax_ell; hence c2 bounded. The order of its 2-local character is <=2^{c2}, and the odd local factors together have order dividing lcm(o,2); hence ord(phi)<=2^{c2}*2o bounded. Choose L=K_phi Q(mu_ell), abelian, totally complex, degree <=(ell-1)ord(phi) uniformly bounded, conductor dividing lcm(N,ell). Conductor-discriminant gives log(2D_L)<=log 2+[L:Q]log(lcm(N,ell))=O_psi(log(2N)). Apply B's restriction bound (using k<=log2 [L:Q]) and GC.D081 with e=r=1. Under Poitou–Tate, the finite Sha1 groups are dual to finite Sha2 groups contained in H2(L,Z/ell^m(2)); these H2 groups are images of integral H2 by higher global vanishing and finite-cohomology inverse limit comparison. For each strict discrete class, finitely many local conditions vanish at some finite coefficient stage (filtered colimits and finite S). Therefore the size of the entire strict discrete kernel is bounded by the integral H2 size, since a direct limit of finite groups of uniformly bounded cardinality has bounded cardinality. By GC.D081, log size <= exp((2[L:Q])^{C_K}log(2D_L)) <= (2N)^C for a fixed C. Thus v log ell <= (2N)^{C'} plus fixed restriction error. Number of characters <=N. The r=1 exception is genuinely needed: q/d factors may survive so fixed tower L' alone is invalid.
