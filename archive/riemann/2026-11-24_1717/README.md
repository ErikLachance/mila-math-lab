## A. Marked normalization and injectivity of orbit-to-isomorphism map
Write F=Qbar_l, H=ker eta=G_K, J antidiagonal ones, M^sharp=J M^t J, theta(M)=(M^sharp)^(-1). At each normalized point r(c)=(I,-1), so for g in H, rho(c g^-1 c)=rho(g)^sharp. Set A=rho(g1). Source imposes A12=A23 and A21=A32. Therefore B=A-A^sharp=diag(d,0,-d), d=A11-A33. Residual diagonal is Psi(g1),1,Psi(g1)^-1, and marked condition ensures d is a unit. Three eigenvalues distinct. Mark existence itself follows by choosing g1 in inertia above ell with mod ell cyclotomic generator: eta trivial on inertia as ell unramified in K; prime-to-ell-conductor component of psi is unramified at ell; psi_ell squared is one. Thus Psi(g1)^2 mod ell is generator^(2e) !=1 by ell-1 not dividing 2e. This proves only marked existence, not the full existential package H9 (g1 must also satisfy automorphy and finiteness).

Suppose two type I normalized rho,rho' are isomorphic via C. Because both satisfy rho(cgc)=theta(rho(g)), the matrices C and theta(C) intertwine the same restrictions. Schur gives theta(C)=lambda C, equivalently C^t J C=mu J for some nonzero mu. Thus sharp commutes with conjugation by C, and B'=C B C^-1. Both B and B' diagonal with eigenvalues d,0,-d; d' must equal d or -d. Both d,d' reduce to the SAME fixed nonzero residual scalar and ell odd, so d'=-d impossible. Hence C commutes with B, is diagonal, and C11 C33=C22^2. Divide by C22 to obtain diag(s,1,s^-1). As r(c) fixed, equality on H extends to full semidirect representation. Evaluation on matrix-entry subalgebra determines the map on S by density/continuity of local points (verify this last source-model property when doing raw Lean audit).

Type II normalized rho has irreducible corner U on span(e1,e3) and middle character chi. Any intertwiner preserves the two blocks by uniqueness of irreducible summands of distinct dimensions. On corner the same Schur argument shows C_corner is J2-similitude. Rescale the intertwiner on middle by a square root of its corner similitude factor (available over algebraically closed F). This changes no intertwining relation and makes entire C a J-similitude. Same B argument now gives torus equivalence. Type I and II cannot be isomorphic. This supplies a concrete multiplicity-control bridge from AGR.1166 orbit representatives to distinct representations required in AGR.1123, subject to established classification and local point density. It does not prove classification or entire AGR.1166. Negative control: remove the marked entry equalities but retain c-normalization, and conjugation by a non-diagonal J-isometry congruent to identity preserves c yet need not lie in torus. Therefore normalization is substantive; do not infer injectivity from Schur alone.

## B. Independent recovery of invariant algebra from trace algebra, conditional H9 finiteness reduction
Let R=S, E=O-algebra generated by entries, S0 weight-zero entry algebra, T=O-algebra generated by tr rho(g) for all g in H. Here rho universal restriction; normalization holds over R. T is contained in S0. B above has d^2=tr B^2/2 in T, because B is difference of two group matrices, so traces of their products are traces of group elements. Define P1=(B^2+dB)/(2d^2), P2=I-B^2/d^2, P3=(B^2-dB)/(2d^2). These are the three fixed diagonal matrix units (as identities in R), using invertible d and 2.

Every invariant product of entries with degrees in {0,+/-1,+/-2} decomposes into individual weight-zero entries and minimal patterns (+1,-1),(+2,-2),(+1,+1,-2),(-1,-1,+2). Proof: remove opposite equal-magnitude pairs; if anything remains then both signs occur and magnitudes differ, so only the triples occur. Reflection sharp turns an entry (i,j) into (4-j,4-i) of same weight in another group matrix. For g outside H, r(g)=r(gc) at matrix level since c normalized. Thus each minimal pattern can be reflected to a directed cycle on vertices 1,2,3: e.g. +1,+1,-2 as 12,23,31. Product of entries on a cycle equals trace(P_i M1 P_j M2 ... P_k Mk). Expanding projectors, each trace is a T[d,d^-1]-linear combination of traces of group products. Weight-zero singleton is trace(P_i M). Hence S0 subset T[d,d^-1], equality after inverting d as needed.

If T finite as O-module, then T is local by complete henselian O and inclusion T subset local R (otherwise nontrivial idempotent). Residue field is same as R because O maps surjectively onto it, and T->R is local (maximal ideal of R contracts to maximal ideal of finite T). In T, Hensel lifts the chosen nonzero residue root of X^2-tr B^2/2 uniquely, since 2d unit. Its image in R equals d (the sum with d is a unit and difference times sum zero). Thus d,d^-1 in T, giving S0=T. This reduces H9 module finiteness to trace algebra module finiteness without separately enumerating all entry products. Even without using Hensel, finite T[d] is local and d unit, so suffices. Not proved that T is finite. Finite many automorphic trace systems alone does not suffice: nonreduced countermodel R=O[[x]][u]/(u^2), T=O+u O[x] is a subring with all characteristic-zero field points killing u, but infinite O-rank. This pinpoints the need for nilpotent/torsion control or a faithful finite evaluation theorem. Do not replace actual ring finiteness by counting closed points. Source D103 uses all algebraic points of E, not merely local points; no unsupported passage.

## C. ALG.1756 (H3) elementary independent proof
Let Q be O-saturation of augmentation ideal squared, C_j image of A_j in A/Q. Since O torsion-free, each integral derivation of degree j kills Q by Leibniz, hence factors through an O-linear form on C_j (degree restriction means values on A_j determine all values). Reduction mod c vanishing on all F_i then gives a unique O-linear form on D=C_j/<F_i> into O/c. The given injective map M of abelian groups thus maps into Hom_O(D,O/c) injectively; no O-linearity of initial map is needed. If length(D)=infinity inequality automatic. Otherwise D direct sum of O/pi^n_i, q=|residue field|, c unit times pi^k. Cardinality of Hom is q^(sum min(n_i,k)) <= q^length(D). Injection makes M finite, and each finite O-module has cardinality q^length(M). This proves length(M)<=length(D), including c unit case. This recovers the catalogue's H3 statement, not just a toy example, using standard DVR structure theorem. It does not establish the crucial arithmetic hypothesis that Selmer classes admit integral lifts. Without integral lift condition, false: A=O[x]/(pi x), deg x=-1, augmentation x=0, Q contains x, C_-1=0, but reduction mod pi has nonzero derivation x->1. For torsion Selmer module V, uniform finite bound on lengths V[pi^k] implies stabilization and length(V) bounded, since each continuous cocycle into discrete torsion coefficients has finite image by compactness, hence is killed by some power. Do not assume all mod-c tangent vectors lift.

## D. Qualitative finiteness of mixed orbits in ALG.0060
Under finite-type hypotheses in ALG.0060, A/I after scalar extension to algebraically closed F is finitely generated: finite-type degree-zero image plus finitely many homogeneous generators. Mixed locus open (at least one nonzero coordinate of each sign). Every mixed orbit has dimension one because nonzero weight coordinate gives finite stabilizer. Such orbit is closed in affine variety: embed via homogeneous generators and degree-zero generators into affine space; for every active coordinate there is opposite-sign coordinate and a monomial invariant with positive powers of both, nonzero at orbit. The nonzero invariant values ensure no active coordinate vanishes in the closure; inactive ones stay zero. In the support torus the image of t->(t^weights) is a closed subtorus (integer lattice kernel, or Smith normal form); hence orbit closed. At each mixed point the hypothesis that every two tangent derivations dependent forces tangent dimension <=1. Local dimension <= tangent dimension, and the orbit is irreducible closed dimension one. Each irreducible component containing the point has dimension <=1 (finite-type over F, closed point); so orbit equals a component containing it (some component contains the irreducible orbit). Noetherian variety finitely many irreducible components => finitely many mixed orbits. This recovers qualitative orbit finiteness without invoking cotangent-length inequality. No proof of factor 2 or length bound from this alone; scheme nilpotents and special fibre integral valuations still require the main ALG.0060 argument. Finite-dimensional numerical tests cannot close that gap.

## E. Rigidity normalization and type-II adjoint splitting
