## New micro-pass 6 — why newer rank-2 modularity cannot be substituted for rank-3 G3

Primary current source: Andrea Dotto and Jack Thorne, *On the modularity of residually reducible Galois representations of rank 2*, arXiv:2610.41764, posted 2026-10-30, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41764 . Rank **2**, totally real fields. Not the same category as the HUMAN reconstruction's generic residually reducible polarized rank **3** unitary automorphy input G3. A tempting shortcut is to view the residual triple `chi^e ⊕ 1 ⊕ chi^(-e)` as the symmetric square of a 2D residual sum. This structural shape ALONE does not ensure characteristic-zero lifts are symmetric squares.

**Exact counterexample to this shortcut**, *not* a global Galois deformation: take prime l=7, e=2, residual chi(g)=2 in F7*. Then normalized residual eigenvalues are chi^2,1,chi^-2 = 4,1,2 mod7. The rational matrix A=diag(4,8,1/32) has determinant 1 and reduces mod7 to precisely diag(4,1,2) (32 mod7=4 inverse2). If A=Sym²(B) for any 2x2 B in characteristic zero, its eigenvalues over algebraic closure must be alpha², alpha*beta, beta², and det(A)=(det B)³. Hence an eigenvalue must have cube equal det(A)=1. None of 4³=64,8³=512,(1/32)³=1/32768 equals 1. So A is NOT Sym²(B), even over algebraic closure. Exact Fraction script `/mnt/data/riemann_20261123_1554/symmetric_square_transfer_obstruction.py`, JSON PASS. Again this is ONE matrix, not a 3-dimensional polarized Galois representation; it only rules out the invalid inference from residual triple to arbitrary rank3 lifts. ## New micro-pass 9 — compatibility of ray-class degree with polynomial conductor penalty
