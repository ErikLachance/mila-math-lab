## Micro-pass G: height-one Artin–Hasse versus height-two Honda log — a rigorous wrong-shortcut obstruction

Fix odd prime p and put N=p². Define height-two Honda logarithm `L_2(T)=sum_{j>=0} T^(p^(2j))/p^j`, and height-one Artin–Hasse logarithm `L_1(T)=sum_{j>=0} T^(p^j)/p^j` over Q_p. In the Dwork condition for ordinary exponential, `L_1(T^p)-p L_1(T)=-pT`, entirely in `pZ_p[[T]]`; therefore `exp(L_1(T))` is p-integral. But at T^(p²), `L_2(T^p)` has NO coefficient, while `p L_2(T)` contributes +1, so Dwork defect equals exactly `-1`, **not divisible by p**. Thus ordinary `exp(L_2(T))` is NOT p-integral. More concretely `coefficient[T^N] exp(epsilon L_2(T)) = epsilon^N/N! + epsilon/p`, and for EVERY p-adic unit epsilon the p-adic valuation is **−(p+1)**, since `v_p((p²)!)=p+1` and numerator `epsilon^N +(N)! epsilon/p` is a unit. Therefore a unit scaling cannot fix naive usual exponential. This does **not** conflict with PHT.0557: it uses the **formal group exponential** `e=f^{-1}` of a *suitable elliptic formal group*, not ordinary exponential.

Exact `fractions.Fraction`/integer regressions for p=3,5,7,11,13 PASS; script `honda_height2_hazewinkel_audit.py/.json`. Infinite-dimensional/formal claims above have independent algebraic proofs, tests only detect arithmetic implementation mistakes. Do not infer either RH or a flaw in Lean.

## Micro-pass H: explicit Honda model, formal group integrality and FULL [p]-series congruence

Let `L(T)=T+T^N/p+T^(N²)/p²+...`, `e=L^{-1}`, `F(X,Y)=e(L(X)+L(Y))`. The exact functional equation `L(T)-L(T^N)/p=T` matches Hazewinkel's integrality lemma over Z_(p) with q=p², Frobenius=id (legitimate because a^(p²)=a modp for every a∈F_p). Thus `F∈Z_(p)[[X,Y]]`. The inverse *itself* is not integral: coefficient `[T^N]e=-1/p`; never conflate integrality of the group law with integrality of its logarithm or exponential.

A NEW self-contained proof of the **entire** reduction `[p]_F(T) ≡ T^(p²) (mod p)`, not just first nonzero coefficient: set `B(Z)=L(pZ)/p = Z+sum_{j>=1}p^(p^(2j)-j-1)Z^(p^(2j))`. All exponents are nonnegative and B has integral coefficients with unit linear term, so its inverse C∈Z_(p)[[T]]. Then `e(pT)=pC(T)∈pZ_(p)[[T]]`. The functional equation rearranges to `pL(T)=pT+L(T^N)`. Hence `[p]_F(T)=e(pL(T))=F(e(pT),T^N)≡ F(0,T^N)=T^N (mod p)`. Consequently height exactly TWO, and `[p^r]_F(T)≡T^(p^(2r)) (mod p)` by iteration. Symbolic exponent sanity p=2,3,5,7,11,13,17 PASS `honda_pseries_full_congruence.py/.json`. This is a local formal-group theorem, not the global Riemann proof.

The degree-N bivariate group-law correction is `F(X,Y)=X+Y-(1/p)sum_{k=1}^{N-1} binom(N,k)X^kY^(N-k) + [terms total degree>N]`. The binomial quotient is integral for every 1≤k<N. It is nonzero modp precisely when p divides k; for k=ap, `binom(p²,ap)/p ≡ (-1)^(a-1)/a (modp)` with 1≤a≤p−1, by expanding `((1+T)^p)^p` modulo p². Checked EXACT integers for five primes, each k. This gives an explicit visible height-two cross term.

## Micro-pass I: unramified quadratic CM multiplier, *exact* scalar action and missing hypothesis

Take **K=Frac W(overline(F_p))**, complete with algebraically closed residue, and σ its Witt Frobenius. K satisfies the residual root condition required by PHT.0557. `Q_{p²}` alone does NOT satisfy this condition because its residue F_(p²) is NOT algebraically closed. Choose Teichmuller unit w of primitive order p+1 in K; then `w^(p²)=w`, `σ²w=w`, `σw=w^p=w^(-1)`, and |σw−w|=1 because their residues differ (p+1>2). The Honda log has `L(wT)=wL(T)` identically, hence `[w]_F(T)=e(wL(T))=wT`, an **exact integral linear action**. The full p-series result supplies a unit coefficient at degree N. Thus the explicit Honda model meets PHT.0557's hypotheses; its conclusion is trivially witnessed by epsilon=1, because e(L(T))=T. This is a toy **model**, not an integral-coordinate comparison for an arbitrary CM elliptic Weierstrass model.

Exact finite-field F_(p²)=F_p[t]/(t²−D) enumeration located a primitive (p+1)-st root w and verified w^p=w^-1, w^(p²)=w and w^p≠w for nine p=3..31; script `honda_unramified_cm_action.py/.json`. Abstract existence follows from cyclicity of finite-field unit groups, not the finite tests. The real bridge remains an *integral conjugacy/coordinate change between the CM elliptic formal group and this Honda model*; merely knowing a curve is supersingular or D^p≡0 modp is NOT enough for that theorem's full conclusion. In any proposed transfer, check the algebraically closed residue hypothesis carefully.

## Micro-pass K: 1×1 theta minor valuation theorem regression, including positive nontrivial cases

New symbolic insight: for even k=2r, the exact slack v−B is `r+(k+1)+(k+1)^2−2/(p²−1)`; for odd k=2r+1 it is `r+[(k+1)+(k+1)^2+1]−1/(p−1)`.
