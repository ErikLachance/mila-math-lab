## A. Corrected archimedean separation without centering the support interval
The 1412 checkpoint used individual |beta| in [q^-1/2,q^1/2]. D051 provides a width-one interval with an unspecified center t, not necessarily zero. Concrete negative control q=2^16, moduli (1024,1/32,1/32), product 1, ratio 32768<q, sum exceeds sqrt(q)+1+1/sqrt(q). These are abstract local numbers, not asserted to be a unitary automorphic parameter. The earlier inference from width and determinant alone must not be used.

New elementary lemma: if real x1,x2,x3 sum to zero and max(x)-min(x)<=1, then sum q^xi <= q^(2/3)+2q^(-1/3) for q>=1. Proof: in the plane sum=0 the polytope has vertices the permutations of (2/3,-1/3,-1/3) and (-2/3,1/3,1/3). A vertex requires at least two linearly independent active differences xi-xj=1; chains of length 2 violate width, so two share the maximum or minimum. Convexity puts the maximum at a vertex. With y=q^(1/3), difference of the two vertex sums is (y-1)^3(y+1)/y^2 >=0. For q>=7, y>7/4, hence sum <(4q+8)/7<q-1-1/q. Last inequality equivalent to 3q^2-15q-7>0 for q>=7.
